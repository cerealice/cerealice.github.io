I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [RFF-CMCC European Institute on Economics and the Environment](https://www.eiee.org) (EIEE) in Milan. I model energy systems to understand how Europe can decarbonize while remaining resilient and competitive.

I completed my PhD at Politecnico di Milano (2023–2026) after an MSc in Energy Engineering at the same university.

Research themes
======
- **Italian power system and carbon neutrality**: investment optimization scenarios and policy responses to the energy crisis.
- **Climate and weather impacts**: weather- and climate-driven supply and demand time series; resilience of the grid to extreme events.
- **European industry transition**: decarbonization without deindustrialization, steel, trade fragmentation and competitiveness.
- **Carbon pricing and policy**: EU CBAM, EU ETS, and the macroeconomic effects of the Fit for 55 package.
- **Demand-side mitigation**: buildings and transport in integrated assessment models.
- **Hydropower in energy models**: hydrologically constrained representation in PyPSA-Eur.
